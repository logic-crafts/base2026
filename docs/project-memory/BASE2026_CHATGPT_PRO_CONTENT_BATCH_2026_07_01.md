# Base2026 AI Visibility Funnel Content Batch

## Executive summary

This content batch positions Alex Yarosh as a practical AI visibility consultant for local service businesses that need to know whether AI search systems can understand, verify, and recommend them. The offer is deliberately simple: start with a Free AI Visibility Snapshot, then move into a deeper diagnostic or implementation sprint only when the business sees what is broken.

The copy avoids hype, fake certainty, and generic SEO language. Each page explains a specific commercial question: Do AI systems mention the business? Do they mention competitors instead? Which pages, profiles, reviews, citations, and source signals can they use? What should be fixed first?

Base2026 is framed as the evidence layer behind the work, not as a separate product that a business owner must decode. The vertical pages use buyer prompts and industry-specific trust signals so they do not read like doorway pages. The pricing page includes two usable variants: one with price placeholders and one without public prices. All unsupported operational details are marked as `[NEEDS FACT]` or `[PRICE NEEDED]` so Hermes can safely map the copy into the existing site.
