## 1. Business context

### Brand / person

Alex Yarosh helps local service businesses understand and improve how they appear across:

- Google organic search
- Google AI Overviews
- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Perplexity
- Bing / Copilot

### Base2026 role

Base2026 is not an agency and not a generic blog. It is a source-backed research layer that collects and organizes public creator-video evidence, SEO/GEO/AEO patterns, source records, topic pages and AI visibility findings.

For public copy, Base2026 should be framed as:

- evidence layer;
- source library;
- research method;
- proof behind the audit;
- not the main thing a local business owner has to understand before taking action.

### Commercial offer

The first offer is a **Free AI Visibility Snapshot**.

It should feel like a useful diagnostic/report, not a vague “contact us” form.

The snapshot checks how a business is represented or ignored by AI search and recommendation surfaces. The output should point to practical next fixes, not promise magic rankings.

## 4. Voice and tone

Write like a sharp, honest consultant who respects the reader’s time.

### Tone rules

- Human, direct, practical.
- Calm confidence, not hype.
- Specific claims over big promises.
- Short paragraphs.
- Clear section headings.
- No corporate fog.
- No generic SaaS copy.
- No exaggerated fear-mongering.
- No “ultimate guide” energy.
- No fake case studies, fake stats, fake testimonials or invented customer results.

### Style rules

- Use active voice.
- Prefer “you” and “your business.”
- Avoid em dashes.
- Avoid filler phrases like “in today’s digital landscape,” “unlock,” “leverage,” “seamless,” “game-changing,” “revolutionize,” “robust,” “cutting-edge.”
- Do not write long SEO walls of text.
- If a page needs detail, use cards, bullets and short explanations.
- If a fact is unknown, write `[NEEDS FACT]` instead of inventing it.

### Brand edge

Alex’s copy can be a little blunt. Light phrases such as “without agency theater” are allowed, but use them sparingly. Do not turn the whole site into a rant.
