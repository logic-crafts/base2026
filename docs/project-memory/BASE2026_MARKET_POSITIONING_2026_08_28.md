# Base2026 Market Positioning

Date: 2026-08-28
Evidence: live product review, official competitor review and DataForSEO packet

## Category

**Open video research engine** and **source-first evidence library for
short-form expert video**.

Base2026 is not a generic video-intelligence platform, an AI-visibility
monitor, a transcript dump or a TikTok downloader. Those categories either
carry the wrong search intent or promise functions the product does not have.

## Public message

**Headline:** Search what experts actually said.

**Subhead:** An open, source-first evidence library for short-form expert
video—searchable, attributable, and built for SEO, GEO and AI-search research.

**One sentence:** Base2026 turns reviewed public expert videos into searchable
evidence records that preserve the creator, original source and correction
path for people and machines.

Cloudflare is product proof and operating methodology, not the hero benefit.

## Users and jobs

- SEO/GEO specialists: find source-backed practitioner observations.
- Researchers and analysts: compare claims without losing provenance.
- Founders and marketers: retrieve practical patterns from expert video.
- Developers and agents: query public-safe records and return to the source.
- Creators: retain attribution and obtain correction/removal paths.

## Competitive landscape

| Group | Examples | Their center of gravity | Base2026 wedge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indexed transcript/video search | Taffy, Yutome | Search or summarize indexed video | Reviewed public evidence, provenance and open access |
| Personal media search | Feedr, Fabric, Quiki | Search a user's saved TikToks/Reels/Shorts | A free public corpus rather than a private library |
| Video AI infrastructure | TwelveLabs, VidNavigator, ManyScripts | APIs and processing primitives | A usable public research product, not infrastructure alone |
| Enterprise video intelligence | BlendVision, Deepgrip | Commercial analytics and enterprise workflows | Narrow, open, attributable research access |
| AI visibility suites | Ahrefs, Semrush, Profound, SE Ranking | Brand visibility monitoring | Source evidence for research; no monitoring claim |
| Native discovery | TikTok Search, Creative Center, Research API | Platform-native discovery and data | Cross-record evidence structure and correction boundary |

## Honest differentiation

- free public research corpus and open-source implementation;
- original-source links, creator attribution and inspectable provenance;
- bounded reviewed excerpts rather than public raw transcripts;
- source, topic and creator discovery surfaces;
- correction, removal and suppression paths;
- public API/JSONL and machine discovery files;
- receipt-gated Cloudflare Workers/D1/R2/Queues/Workflows pipeline;
- search does not require a live LLM call for each visitor.

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim “first”, “only”, “all TikTok”, complete coverage, real-time
coverage, perfect transcription, guaranteed rankings/citations, live MCP or a
general AI-visibility platform. The corpus is curated and the current product
is retrieval with attributable excerpts.

## Visibility wedge

The fastest credible route is not thousands of thin pages. Publish a small set
of strong topic evidence maps backed by the existing corpus, link them to
source/creator pages and expose the same objects in the sitemap, API and
machine-readable files. Target product-fit queries around AI video search,
TikTok research and content intelligence while rejecting downloader,
login/troubleshooting and surveillance intent.

## Search evidence

The 2026-08-28 US/en DataForSEO packet cost $0.077. Useful directional terms
included `ai video search`, `tiktok search engine`, `content intelligence
platform`, `ai visibility platform` and `ai search visibility tools`.
`video intelligence platform` was rejected as the main category because the
observed SERP centered on surveillance and multimodal enterprise APIs. Broad
ideas skewed toward downloader and troubleshooting intent and were rejected.
