# Apply Base2026 Research to a Business Visibility Audit

Base2026 is the public research and proof layer. It helps operators inspect source-backed patterns across SEO, GEO, AEO, AI search, service-page content, entity trust, citations, schema, internal links and answer-ready pages.

Use Base2026 to study public patterns. Use Alex Yarosh's audit workflow to diagnose a specific business.

This page explains where the public research layer ends and where business-specific SEO/GEO/AEO work begins.

## What Base2026 can do

Base2026 can help you inspect public source intelligence:

- repeated SEO/GEO/AEO topics;
- creator-backed source records;
- exact source passages;
- topic evidence pages;
- creator profiles;
- source-intelligence cards;
- patterns around schema, internal links, service pages, citations, local SEO and AI search visibility.

It is useful when you want to understand how practitioners discuss visibility problems before applying that research to a business.

## What Base2026 is not

Base2026 is not:

- a lead database;
- a local business profile system;
- a private client vault;
- a replacement for original creators;
- a place to upload credentials, raw client transcripts or confidential strategy;
- a sales page pretending to be a research product.

Keep the public layer public. Route private business diagnosis through the Alex Yarosh audit path.

## When to use this page

Use this bridge when a business owner, founder, marketing lead or SEO operator wants to turn public research patterns into practical site work.

### If visibility gaps are unclear

The first business-specific step is a compact visibility check: establish whether the business can be found, understood and compared across search and AI-powered discovery before prescribing implementation.

### If the business needs a deeper roadmap

A deeper diagnostic is appropriate before investing in SEO, content, citations or a redesign. It should turn the public patterns into a business-specific sequence, not another generic checklist.

### If technical blockers are already likely

The implementation scope may need crawl, indexation, schema, canonical, internal-link and page-structure work. Confirm those blockers in the business-specific diagnosis before choosing the implementation layer.

### If service pages are weak

The implementation scope may need clearer service definitions, buyer questions, FAQs, proof, local context and conversion paths. The evidence should determine which pages deserve rebuilding.

### If the business is hard to verify

The implementation scope may need stronger business-entity clarity, citations, profiles, reviews, proof pages, source signals and schema. Verify the missing trust layer before recommending more publishing.

## Recommended workflow

### 1. Search the public source layer

Use Base2026 to inspect topics such as internal linking, schema, answer-ready content, local SEO, AI Overviews, ChatGPT SEO and crawl/indexation problems.

### 2. Identify the business question

Do not start with "we need SEO." Start with the actual visibility problem: not indexed, not recommended, weak service pages, inconsistent citations, poor reviews, unclear entity, competitor advantage or missing proof.

### 3. Route private diagnosis into the audit path

A business-specific recommendation needs the website, market, competitors, services, locations and public footprint. That work belongs in the Alex Yarosh audit workflow.

### 4. Choose the implementation layer

After diagnosis, route the work into technical SEO, answer-ready service pages, entity trust, citations, monitoring or a broader visibility sprint.

## Public boundary

Do not send private source vaults, raw transcripts, credentials, customer lists, analytics exports or lead data through Base2026.

Base2026 remains the public source-intelligence layer. Alex Yarosh's site remains the conversion, audit and service layer.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Base2026?

Base2026 is a searchable public research product that turns attributed short-form expert video records into source passages, topic pages, creator profiles and SEO/GEO/AEO research patterns.

### Can Base2026 audit my business?

No. Base2026 can show public research patterns. A private business audit requires website, competitor, market, service and trust-signal context through the Alex Yarosh audit workflow.

### Can I use Base2026 for SEO, GEO and AEO research?

Yes. Use it to inspect recurring patterns around technical SEO, schema, internal links, content structure, AI-search visibility, local SEO, citations and answer-ready pages.

### Should I upload private data to Base2026?

No. Keep private business materials, credentials, transcripts, lead data and client documents outside Base2026. Use the appropriate business workflow for private diagnosis.

### What is the best next step after research?

If the business problem is unclear, start with the free AI Visibility Snapshot. If the business needs a deeper roadmap, request the AI Visibility Diagnostic Audit.
