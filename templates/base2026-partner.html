Partner with Base2026

Work on a source-backed research layer.

Base2026 is looking for practical collaborations that make public evidence easier to find, verify, cite and correct. The best partnerships start with a narrow, testable contribution and transparent boundaries.

Infrastructure and open source

Hosting, databases, observability, security, developer tooling and maintainable operations.

Data and research tooling

Discovery, provenance, extraction, evaluation and verification workflows.

Research and education

Universities, newsrooms, labs and communities studying information discovery and AI search.

Rights and creator support

Attribution, provenance, corrections, opt-out systems and creator-respectful discovery.

Partnership principles

  • Attribution and traceability come first.
  • No pay-to-rank, editorial control or private-data access.
  • Scope, data boundaries and public acknowledgment are agreed in advance.
  • We prefer a small first step with a clear success check and rollback.

Propose a partnership

A concise proposal is enough. Please do not submit credentials, confidential information or private datasets.

Submissions are stored outside the public research index and reviewed only for project operations.
