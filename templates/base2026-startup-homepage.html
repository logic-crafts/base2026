The feed is not
a knowledge base.

Short-form platforms are designed for consumption velocity, not research accuracy. Expert observations are difficult to search, compare, cite, and revisit.

Base2026 creates a searchable, attributed research layer without replacing the original source, turning transient scrolls into permanent evidence.

Our Thesis

  • Source-linked evidence makes AI research auditable.
  • Attribution builds durable trust for knowledge tools.
  • Reviewed structured data supports more reliable AI agents.

Trust as infrastructure.

Attribution First

Every record links back to the original source. We do not re-host creator videos or package creator work as a private vault.

Reviewed Context

Search results are based on reviewed public text, not raw, unverified transcript dumps.

Clear Boundaries

Visible correction/removal paths and strict public methodology guide every entry.

Data Sovereignty

Private artifacts and credentials are excluded from the public API and index by design.

Current Policy

Source Excerpt Policy

Base2026 operates as a discovery layer. We strictly adhere to an excerpt-only policy for public short-form content to ensure creators retain their primary viewing traffic.
