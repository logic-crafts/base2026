Source policy

Public evidence with clear rights and correction paths.

Base2026 is an independent research and discovery project. Records are based on public material and are provided for search, attribution and research purposes.

Selection and allowed sources

Base2026 is selected by its founder and team for practical relevance, usefulness and source provenance. We do not accept paid creator placement or creator applications. Records may be based on publicly accessible short-form expert videos, public creator posts and captions, public educational or professional material, creator-authorized sources and manually reviewed public materials. A publicly submitted URL may support a correction request or research feedback; submitting a URL is not an admission request and does not guarantee inclusion.

Disallowed sources

Base2026 should not intentionally collect or publish private or login-restricted content, paywalled or leaked material, private messages, sensitive personal content unrelated to public expert discussion, content where removal has been requested or content obtained by bypassing access controls.

Attribution and display

Public records should preserve the creator or handle, platform, original source URL, publication date when available, source-text status and correction or review status. Reviewed public source text may be shown when policy allows, alongside the original link and Base2026 context. Raw captions, raw ASR, downloaded media, extraction logs and full private transcripts remain private by default.

AI and editorial use

AI may help transcribe, classify, find related records or improve search. It must not fabricate claims or present unsupported statements as facts. Any answer built from the database should link back to the attributed source record. Selection is not certification that every claim is correct.

Correction and removal

Creators may request attribution fixes, source-text corrections, record removal, future suppression or an official creator link. Send a request to Creator rights and removal or offflinerpsy@gmail.com. Reasonable requests are reviewed in good faith. The founder and team may also decline, defer or remove a record when source support, rights or public usefulness do not meet this policy.

Platform respect

Base2026 does not bypass private access controls or platform security. It prefers public URLs, authorized sources, conservative rate limits, bounded private processing and transparent provenance.
