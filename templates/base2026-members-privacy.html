Optional Google sign-in and My Research

Public search, sources and articles do not require an account. If you choose Google sign-in, Base2026 uses your basic Google identity to create and secure your account. It does not request access to Gmail or Drive, or permission to act on your behalf in other Google services.

My Research stores your account identity, sessions, named collections, sources you explicitly save and notes you write in a separate private database operated through Cloudflare. These records are not added to public search, source pages, datasets or editorial publishing. Public searches are not automatically saved as account history. Do not put passwords, credentials or confidential third-party information in notes.

An essential, secure session cookie keeps you signed in. Temporary sign-in state can include the page URL needed to return you to your workspace. It becomes unusable after expiry; expired state and hashed abuse-control records are removed in bounded batches during authentication activity, so inactive records may remain until later cleanup. These records are separate from account research history and recommendation profiles. Google handles its own authentication and privacy practices.

In My Research, you can export your saved data, remove individual items or collections, sign out, revoke all Base2026 sessions, or delete your account after a recent sign-in. Account deletion removes active account, session, collection and note records. Earlier copies may remain in service backups until their retention period expires. Deleting a Base2026 account does not delete your Google account.
