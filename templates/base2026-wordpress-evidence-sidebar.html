A small research loop

Keep research beside your draft.

Type a focused topic, inspect what the public source actually says, and choose what belongs in your draft. The sidebar keeps the decision in Gutenberg without pretending to fact-check or write for you.

What the beta does

Three deliberate actions.

No signup, API key or paid AI service is required. Search is a read-only public lookup; nothing is autosent from a post.

01 / SEARCH

Ask one focused question

Enter a topic or question up to 160 characters. The text goes to Search only when you choose Search; it is not silently sent while you edit a post.

02 / REVIEW

Read the source card

Open the public record, see its attribution and follow the original source where available before you reuse an idea.

03 / INSERT

Choose the editable note

Insert a research note only when you want it. The note stays editable, and the optional Base2026 link is opt-in rather than forced into every post.

Install in three steps

From ZIP to your first source card.

Get the beta ZIP ↗
  1. 01

    Install the ZIP

    Download the beta, then in WordPress open Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin and choose the ZIP.

  2. 02

    Activate it

    Activate Base2026 Evidence Sidebar. There is no Base2026 account, browser API key or paid AI setup step.

  3. 03

    Open Base2026 Evidence

    Open a post in the block editor, open the Base2026 Evidence sidebar, and search only when you are ready to review a public record.

Before you publish

You still own the claim.

The inserted note is attributed and editable, not a guaranteed direct quotation and not a fact check. Read the original source, correct the wording and decide whether to publish it.

The beta does not autosend post content, publish for you, promise rankings or upload private drafts. Keep private client context and confidential research outside the public search.
