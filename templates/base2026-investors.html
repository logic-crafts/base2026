Investor overview

Video knowledge, ready for your next question.

Built by Alex Yarosh, an SEO practitioner turning a recurring research problem into a product.

The opportunity Make useful practitioner knowledge reusable.

The free product starts with finding sources. Our next test is whether a focused professional workflow helps people return, compare and act.

Product

A searchable record between a practitioner’s post and a research decision.

Attributed records

Base2026 organizes selected practitioner material into attributed, searchable records. People can inspect sources, follow originals, use practical free checks and retain selected source references in My Research.

Useful context

Selection favors practical usefulness and provenance. A record keeps the creator’s statement close to Base2026 context and makes unresolved questions visible.

Bounded briefs

The public brief builder organizes chosen records into a local research scaffold. It does not determine which recommendation is correct or replace a professional’s judgment.

Problem

A narrow question can be buried in hours of videos, posts and documents.

When someone needs to compare what practitioners actually said about SEO or AI search, watching one video gives one perspective and a generic summary can hide who said what. The original context is easy to lose while the research time accumulates.

Base2026 focuses on the retrieval and provenance layer: search selected records, compare useful passages, open the original source and decide what deserves a deeper investigation.

Difference

Selection is part of the product, so its limits stay visible.

Provenance first

Original links, creator attribution and a bounded source record stay close to every useful passage.

Source and context

We keep a creator’s statement next to Base2026 editorial qualification and unresolved questions instead of presenting an extracted claim as a verdict.

Question to source

Selection is for practical relevance, usefulness and provenance. It helps a reader move from a real question to an original source without implying creator endorsement.

Technology

Structured records make the path from question to source inspectable.

A coordinated pipeline supports source discovery, review and the conversion of permitted video material into structured text. Search connects a question to attributed records; public API and MCP access make that research layer usable from other tools.

We built AgencyOS, a private operating environment that helps a solo founder coordinate development, research and publishing. Explore the public API, MCP connection or the factory scenario.

Investment hypothesis

Test recurring demand around a concrete research job.

The commercial hypothesis starts with small SEO practices that repeatedly research recommendations for clients. A pilot would test repeat use, willingness to pay and the cost of delivering useful research. The public search and tools remain free.

Investment could help expand editorial capacity or a professional workflow once usage and paid demand identify the next constraint. Pilot demand, renewal and delivery economics are the milestones to establish.

Selection disclosure

A public record needs a way back to its source.

Sources are selected by the founder or team for practical relevance, usefulness and provenance. We do not accept paid creator placement or creator applications.

Selection is not certification that every claim is correct. Original sources remain linked, and corrections or removal requests are welcome. Coverage and publication timing depend on review.
