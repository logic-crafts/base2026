Methodology

Keep the source close to the useful passage.

Base2026 turns selected public practitioner material into attributed records that can be searched, read, compared and cited. The original creator source remains the canonical place to watch or read the full work.

Select

We select public material for practical relevance, usefulness and provenance. Selection is an editorial choice, not a claim that every available source is covered.

Attribute

Each record keeps the creator or public handle, platform, original URL and publication date when available. If attribution cannot be established, the record should not be promoted.

Review

Reviewed source text is normalized for readability and bounded into useful passages. A creator’s statement, Base2026 context and unresolved questions remain distinct.

Correct

Records retain correction and removal paths. A source can be challenged, corrected, suppressed or removed without turning the public index into a private transcript vault.

What the public layer contains

Public pages may contain reviewed source text, short evidence excerpts, attribution, topic organization and Base2026-authored explanation. The public brief builder creates a local research scaffold from chosen records; it does not decide which recommendation is correct.

Private processing may assist with bounded discovery, transcription, classification and review. The public layer exposes the original source, the useful context and the available correction path; internal processing details remain outside the public website and API. See the source policy for selection, attribution, correction and removal boundaries.

What a record does not prove

A selected record is not a survey, a truth score, a ranking of creators or proof that a tactic will work for a particular business. A source match can be adjacent to a question. Compare multiple records, inspect the original and document what remains unknown.
