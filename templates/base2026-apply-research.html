Research scope

Use public evidence without turning it into a private client vault.

Base2026 is a public research layer for checking attributed source material, recurring topics and documented methods. It is not a business audit service or a place to upload confidential information.

What the public layer is for

Search sources, compare how creators describe a question, inspect the original attribution and use topic pages to form a bounded research hypothesis. Cite the source record or original creator when sharing a finding.

Keep private work outside Base2026

Do not place credentials, customer lists, analytics exports, raw private transcripts, internal strategy or unpublished work in the public research layer. Public evidence may identify a question worth investigating; the decision and its private inputs belong elsewhere.

A practical research sequence

  1. Search for the question, creator or method.
  2. Open the attributed source and retain its limits.
  3. Compare repeated signals instead of treating one short video as proof.
  4. Document what remains unknown before deciding on the next investigation.
