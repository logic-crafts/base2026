About Base2026

A public research layer with a way back to the source.

Base2026 is an independent, founder-led open-source project. It makes selected practitioner knowledge easier to find, inspect and use while keeping attribution, context and limitations visible.

Why it exists

Useful practitioner knowledge is scattered across short videos, posts and documents. Base2026 helps a researcher start with a narrow question, find an attributed record and follow the original source.

What the product does

Selected public material becomes searchable records, source pages, topic and creator views, practical free checks and local research scaffolds. The original source remains canonical.

How selection works

We select for practical relevance, usefulness and provenance. A creator’s statement, Base2026 editorial context and unresolved questions remain distinct.

Where the boundary is

People should be able to follow the original source, understand the Base2026 context and request a correction or removal. A public record is not a truth certification or creator endorsement.

How we grow

Show the work where practitioners already learn.

Alex publishes avatar videos on YouTube and TikTok. Base2026 also shares practical research through X, LinkedIn, original articles and useful free tools. Demonstrations show a real question, the product, the original source and the limits of a finding. Instagram remains a future experiment.

Selection disclosure

Useful does not mean unquestionable.

We do not accept paid creator placement or creator applications. Selection is not certification that every claim is correct. Original sources remain linked, and corrections or removal requests are welcome.
