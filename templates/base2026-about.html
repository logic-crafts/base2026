About Base2026

Public evidence for people and AI systems that need to check the source.

Base2026 is an independent open-source startup that converts public expert videos into structured, attributed research records. It is built by solo founder Alex Yarosh and developed in the open.

Why it exists

Useful expert knowledge is scattered across short-form platforms and difficult to retrieve later. Base2026 makes reviewed public evidence searchable without replacing the original creator or source.

Who it is for

Researchers, marketers, SEO and GEO specialists, founders, developers and AI agents that need traceable evidence instead of unsupported summaries.

How it works

Public sources are captured, reviewed, attributed, split into useful passages and connected to creator, topic and methodology pages. The original source remains canonical.

What it is not

It is not an agency funnel, a pay-to-rank directory, a private transcript vault or a replacement for creator channels. Private pipeline material stays private.

Built as open infrastructure

The public repository documents the software and operating boundaries. The roadmap focuses on provenance, correction workflows, transparent evaluation and developer access. Base2026 welcomes startup-program support and scoped partnerships that preserve editorial independence.
