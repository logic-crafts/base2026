# Base2026 Product Marketing Context

Version: 1.0 · 2026-08-28

## Product

Base2026 is an open-source, free public video research engine. It turns
reviewed public short-form expert video into searchable, attributable evidence
for humans and AI systems.

## Core audience

SEO/GEO practitioners, researchers, analysts, founders, marketers, developers
and source-conscious AI-agent builders. Creators are a rights-bearing
stakeholder, not inventory.

## Problem

Expert knowledge in short-form feeds is difficult to search, compare, revisit
and cite. Generic summaries commonly lose the original speaker, date and
context.

## Positioning

- Category: open video research engine / source-first evidence library.
- Headline: Search what experts actually said.
- Benefit: retrieve useful expert evidence without losing provenance.
- Proof: public search, source/topic/creator pages, API/JSONL, correction
  paths and a documented Cloudflare evidence pipeline.

## Differentiation

Free public corpus; reviewed excerpts rather than raw transcript dumping;
original-source attribution; open-source access; human- and machine-readable
surfaces; receipt-gated Cloudflare Workers/D1/R2/Queues/Workflows operations.

## Competitor frames

- Personal saved-video search is private-library first.
- Video-AI infrastructure sells processing primitives.
- Enterprise intelligence sells analytics/workflows.
- AI-visibility suites monitor brands in answer engines.
- Base2026 provides a narrow public evidence layer for expert video.

## Objections and answers

- “Why not TikTok search?” Base2026 structures evidence across records and
  keeps source/correction context visible.
- “Is this a transcript dump?” No. Public output is reviewed, bounded and
  source-linked; raw media/transcripts remain private.
- “Is it complete?” No. It is curated and transparent about coverage.
- “Why Cloudflare?” It makes the public product and bounded pipeline durable
  and inexpensive; it is evidence of the method, not the main user benefit.

## Voice

Direct, technically credible, source-conscious and plain. Prefer specific
proof over superlatives. Never imply creator endorsement, universal coverage,
perfect transcription, guaranteed SEO results or functions not yet live.

## Current growth goal

Earn discovery for a small group of product-fit research intents through
excellent evidence maps, technically sound indexation, consistent entity
language and public machine access. Avoid thin programmatic pages and unrelated
high-volume downloader/troubleshooting traffic.
